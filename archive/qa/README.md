## Purpose & Scope

This directory contains archived pre-production audit reports, legacy phase test passes (Pass 01 - Pass 05, Phases B - F), UX research notes, and interim simulation logs generated during early development phases of the Open Trade & Procurement (OTP) platform.

These documents are preserved strictly for historical, architectural, and regulatory compliance audit trails.

> **Note for Active Platform Development & Testing:**  
> For current, active technical specifications, refer to the canonical master documentation suite in [`docs/`](../../docs/00-DOCUMENTATION-INDEX.md).  
> For current active release checklists and operational runbooks, refer to [`qa/release/`](../../qa/release/) and [`docs/STANDALONE-OPERATIONS-RUNBOOK.md`](../../docs/STANDALONE-OPERATIONS-RUNBOOK.md).
