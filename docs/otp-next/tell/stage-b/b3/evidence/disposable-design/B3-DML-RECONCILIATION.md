# Source-Checked DML Reconciliation

## Verdict and method

The Phase 0C documents reported **293 direct migration-time DML statements**
(148 INSERT, 123 UPDATE, 22 DELETE). Phase 0E found an additional explicit
migration-time call in `00020_taxonomy_backfill.sql:342`:
`SELECT public.backfill_taxonomy();`. The called function contains 13 DML
statement sites (6 UPDATE requirements, 4 INSERTs into supplier capability
and service-area tables, 1 UPDATE supplier_capabilities, and 2 UPDATE
suppliers) at lines 100, 106, 119, 128, 140, 179, 204, 255, 265, 278, 294,
310, and 323. Those statements execute as part of migration 00020, subject
to their predicates and source data. The safe generated-stream rule is to
remove the explicit call, not the function definition.

The two `00138_seed_contact_supplier_logins.sql:228,247` INSERTs remain
deferred: they are inside `private.notify_suppliers_of_outcome(uuid)`, which
is defined but not invoked by that migration. The reconciled inventory is
therefore **308 DML-like source locations**, comprising 293 direct statements,
13 invoked function-body statements, and 2 uninvoked stored-function-body
statements. Effective migration-time DML source sites total **306**:
152 INSERT, 132 UPDATE, and 22 DELETE. These are SQL statement sites, not
affected-row counts.

This reconciliation enumerates the statement locations in
`B3-UNSAFE-MIGRATION-STATEMENT-REGISTER.md`; it does not claim that a lexical
inventory proves absence of unregistered dynamic SQL or procedural effects.
`DO` block bodies execute at migration time. **79 of the 293 direct
migration-time statements are inside executable DO blocks** and must be
treated as migration-time operations, not deferred function bodies. The 13
statements reached by the explicit `backfill_taxonomy()` call are a separate
execution context.

No row values, predicates, credentials, personal values, hashes, tokens, supplier/customer names, or transaction payloads are reproduced. All reported DML is row data/configuration mutation, not structurally necessary DDL. Some reference/backfill rows may be semantic prerequisites or preserve invariants; their omission can make behavior incomplete and must not be disguised as a complete application replay.
