## 2. KEY STRATEGIC ADVANTAGES OF OTP

### 2.1. Structural Anti-Corruption & Bias Elimination

- **OTP Advantage (PA-01 / PA-02):** Uncorrelatable Crockford Base32 aliases (`SUPP-7W4K`) completely isolate evaluator scoring from vendor identity until the final award decision receipt is cryptographically sealed.

### 2.2. Zero Supplier Margin Tax
- **Conventional Marketplaces (Udaan / IndiaMART):** Charge heavy listing subscriptions or take 5-15% margin cuts, forcing suppliers to inflate prices.
- **OTP Advantage:** Suppliers quote for **₹0 lead fees** with 0% registration charge. Platform takes a transparent **0.50% settlement fee** upon successful PO fulfillment, conserving genuine wholesale commercial pricing for buyers.

### 2.3. Zero-App WhatsApp Quoting
- **Conventional:** Small Indian manufacturers and regional vendors resist creating complex vendor portal logins.
- **OTP Advantage:** Vendors receive structured RFQs over WhatsApp, reply with pricing and delivery timelines, and OTP's deterministic parser automatically formats and ingests line items.
